# Code of conduct

lazybrick is a technical open-source project. Participation requires direct,
respectful, evidence-based collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Critique claims, experiments, and code rather than people.
- Give actionable feedback and acknowledge uncertainty or mistakes.
- Respect different experience levels, identities, backgrounds, and communication styles.
- Credit prior work and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Protect private information, credentials, unpublished data, and vulnerability details.
- Correct misleading benchmark, authorship, licensing, or provenance claims promptly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
doxxing, sustained disruption, impersonation, plagiarism, benchmark
manipulation, fabricated evidence, and publishing another person's private
information without permission are not tolerated.

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, project spaces, and
public interactions where someone represents lazybrick.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not post sensitive conduct reports publicly. Contact a maintainer through
their GitHub profile and request a private channel without including private
details in the initial message. Security vulnerabilities use `SECURITY.md`.

Maintainers will review available evidence, avoid unnecessary disclosure, and
act proportionately. Actions may include a private correction, warning, content
removal, temporary participation restriction, or permanent removal. A
maintainer with a conflict of interest should recuse themselves when another
maintainer is available.

Good-faith disagreement, negative experimental results, and rigorous criticism
are welcome. Bad-faith misrepresentation and attacks are not.
